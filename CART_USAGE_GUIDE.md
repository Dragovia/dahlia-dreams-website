# 🛒 Cart Usage Guide - Dahlia Dreams

## How to Use Your Shopping Cart

Your Dahlia Dreams flower shop has a complete shopping cart system! Here's how to use all the features:

## 📱 **Accessing Your Cart**

### **Step 1: Add Items to Cart**
1. Browse the flower collection on the main page
2. Click **"Add to Cart"** on any flower you want
3. You'll see a success notification
4. The cart icon in the top right will show the number of items

### **Step 2: Open Your Cart**
- Click the **🛒 cart icon** in the top right navigation
- A sliding sidebar will open from the right side
- This shows all your chosen items

## 🛍️ **Cart Features**

### **View Your Items**
The cart sidebar shows:
- **Flower image** (emoji)
- **Flower name**
- **Price per item**
- **Quantity controls**
- **Remove button**

### **Manage Quantities**
For each item, you can:
- **Increase quantity**: Click the **+** button
- **Decrease quantity**: Click the **-** button
- **Set exact quantity**: Type a number in the input field
- **Remove item**: Click the **"Remove"** button

### **Real-time Total**
- The total price updates automatically as you change quantities
- Shows at the bottom of the cart: **"Total: $XX.XX"**

## 🎯 **Cart Actions**

### **Remove Individual Items**
- Click the **"Remove"** button next to any item
- Item disappears immediately
- Total updates automatically
- Success notification appears

### **Clear Entire Cart**
- Click the **"Clear Cart"** button at the bottom
- Removes all items at once
- Cart becomes empty
- Success notification appears

### **Proceed to Checkout**
- Click the **"Checkout"** button
- Opens checkout form with shipping details
- Shows order summary
- Complete your purchase

## 🔄 **How It Works**

### **Before Sign In**
- Items are saved in your browser's local storage
- Cart persists between page refreshes
- You can add items and manage quantities

### **After Sign In**
- Cart syncs with your Firebase account
- Items are saved to the cloud
- Cart is available across devices
- Orders are tracked in your account

## 📱 **Mobile Experience**

On mobile devices:
- Cart sidebar takes full screen width
- Touch-friendly buttons
- Easy quantity controls
- Responsive design

## 🎨 **Visual Features**

### **Cart Icon Badge**
- Shows total number of items
- Updates in real-time
- Red circle with white number

### **Sliding Animation**
- Smooth slide-in from right
- Overlay darkens background
- Professional feel

### **Success Notifications**
- Green notifications for successful actions
- Red notifications for errors
- Auto-dismiss after 3 seconds

## 🛠️ **Troubleshooting**

### **Cart Not Opening**
- Make sure you're on `localhost:5002`
- Check browser console for errors (F12)
- Try refreshing the page

### **Items Not Adding**
- Make sure you're signed in
- Check if Firebase is connected
- Look for error notifications

### **Cart Not Saving**
- Check if you're signed in
- Verify Firebase connection
- Try signing out and back in

## 🎉 **Quick Tips**

1. **Add multiple items**: Click "Add to Cart" on different flowers
2. **Change quantities**: Use +/- buttons or type directly
3. **Remove items**: Click "Remove" button on any item
4. **View total**: Cart total updates automatically
5. **Checkout**: Sign in first, then click "Checkout"
